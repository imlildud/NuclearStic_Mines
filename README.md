## About

<img width="477" height="242" alt="features" src="assets/hud/features.png" />

Nuclear-Stic Mines is a **turn-based tactical roguelike** that blends classic Minesweeper mechanics with procedural exploration, unique character abilities, and intense rescue missions.

Navigate through hazardous zones, flag hidden dangers, rescue lost children, and bring them back safely to the shelter. Each decision matters — one wrong step could be your last in the wasteland.

---

## How to Play

<img width="200" height="200" alt="legacy" src="assets/hud/menu/legacy.png" />

| Action | Control |
|--------|---------|
| **Move** | `WASD` or Touch pad |
| **Enteract** | `Arrow Keys` (Direction) or Touch pad |
| **Change enteract mode** | `F` or Swamp button|
| **Pause Game** | `ESC` |
| **Rescue Mission** | Step on a pal, then safely return to the starting house |

**Objective:** Locate and rescue all pal marked as *Wanted* on the map and deliver them safely back to the starting tile.

## Game Modes

<img width="477" height="242" alt="gamemodes" src="assets/hud/gamemodes.png" />

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Legacy** | Progressive difficulty. Each victory advances to a higher hazard level. Tracks your personal records. |
| **Hardcore** | Extreme survival version of Legacy. No health regeneration, limited flag capacity, forced Ash Zone environment. |
| **Daily** | A fixed seeded mission synchronized for all players worldwide. You only get **one attempt** per day. |
| **Custom** | Complete control over your wasteland. Adjust grid size, hazards, obstacles, mission goals, visual zone, and specific seeds. |

## Key Mechanics

<img width="200" height="200" alt="options" src="assets/hud/menu/options.png" />

| Mechanic | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Dynamic Vision** | Fog of War active. Only tiles within your character's vision range are revealed. Mountain tiles block your direct line of sight. |
| **Terrain Height** | Verticality matters. You cannot climb more than 1 level up at once. Falling 2+ levels down deals environmental damage. |
| **Tactical Flags** | Mark suspected hazard tiles. Once a moving hazard is successfully flagged, it becomes trapped and stops moving. |
